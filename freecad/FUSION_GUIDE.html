Printing constraint

Both gray cradles/arms are frozen because printing has started. Do not change shared arm dimensions. Teal outlet-rail refinements must fit the frozen arm baseline in frozen_print_arms/.

Appearance and recovery

Matte teal, graphite and gold with dark outlines; solid background #9EA6AD. The local workspace module reapplies the theme and ribbon at startup. Preferences and layout backups live beside the model. Keep app error reporting available through View → Panels → Report view; logs are captured by the model's local runner.
