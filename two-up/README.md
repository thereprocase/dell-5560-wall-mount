# Two-Up: two Precision 5560s on one wall mount

A two-up variant of Minimalist M1.1: two tilted cradles stacked at a 260 mm pitch, fed from a single rear plenum, with flat cheek shrouds closing the sides. 38 printed parts plus a fit coupon, each sized for a 256 mm bed.

**Status:** CAD, FreeCAD B-rep measurements and automated bed-fit and interference checks only. No part has been printed or test-fitted.

- [3D viewer and downloads](https://thereprocase.github.io/dell-5560-wall-mount/two-up/)
- [Design guidance](DESIGN_GUIDANCE.md): the layout reasoning and estimates behind every parameter
- [Build and check scripts](work/README.md): `params.py`, the FreeCAD builder, `check.py` and the verify scripts; `export/` holds the STEP and STL files in print orientation
