# Modular Laptop Cooling Wall Mount

An open wall mount with separate laptop arms, removable 120 mm fan holders, and optional adjustable air guides. Choose a measured size preset, then set both air guides to the same height.

Minimalist M1.1 removes the full ducts and keeps the parts independent. The reinforced arms use wider wall pads and tapered webs at the wall junction. The fan holders, caps, keys, and retainers can be replaced separately.

**Early-adopter prototype:** complete physical fit, retention, warm creep, and cooling remain unqualified. Start with the fit coupon and a supported trial assembly.

## Choose by case dimensions

Measure the closed laptop, including its feet. Check the ports, vents, and cable bends. The size labels are examples, not universal fit claims.

## Geometry that matters

The reference design leaves a 40 mm wall gap and uses separate 2 mm contact pads. Two 120 × 120 × 27 mm fan envelopes sit at 45° and direct air upward into that gap.

Each optional air guide has nine positions, numbered 0–8, at 12 mm pitch. Its key spans two rungs, or 24 mm. Use matching positions on both sides. The supplied installed render shows position 8.

Leave space for at least 43 mm of upward laptop movement when removing it. A 25 mm fan needs suitable spacing in the modeled 27 mm seat. Check the fan body, wire exit, and connector before assembly.

M1.1 widens the wall pads to 32 mm. Paired 6 mm webs taper 16 mm forward from the wall junction. Nominal sliding clearance is 0.30 mm. The split retainers have intentional interference; print the fit coupon before the full set.

## Parts and printing

For the complete set, print parts 01–08 and hardware plate 21. Plate 21 already contains hardware 09–20. Do not print that hardware twice. Without the optional air guides, use parts 01–06 and hardware plate 22.

The Bambu Studio projects currently cover the 5560 reference size. Other measured presets are supplied as separate model files. Do not scale a complete assembly to obtain another fit: scaling also changes joints, fan seats, and fastener holes.

The new profiles use 0.20 mm layers, four walls, six top and bottom layers, and 20% gyroid infill. Separate projects use Bambu PLA Basic, PETG HF, or ASA. Preserve the supplied build directions. Mount the arms level and parallel with anchors suited to the wall and load.

## Development status

This design has CAD assembly and virtual print review. It has not yet been physically printed or tested with a laptop. The new print profiles are also untested physically. There is no validated load, creep, retention, or cooling rating.

The gallery contains CAD renders and a simplified laptop envelope. Physical test results and revisions will be added as development continues. Fit reports are welcome; include the preset, laptop dimensions, filament, printer, and revision.
