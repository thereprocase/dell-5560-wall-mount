# 15.6-inch Laptop Ducted Cooling Wall Mount

A wall mount for a closed laptop, with two 120 mm fans and ducts that direct air toward its underside. The laptop lifts out for access. The fan trays slide out for service.

This is the full ducted design, Revision H. It uses the Dell Precision 5560 as its dimensional reference. Other laptops can fit when their case dimensions, feet, ports, and vents match the available space. Screen size alone does not establish fit.

**Early-adopter prototype:** complete physical fit, retention, warm creep, and cooling remain unqualified. Start with a fit coupon and a supported trial assembly.

## Fit and geometry

| Feature | Dimension or function |
|---|---|
| Reference laptop | 344.4 × 230.3 × 20 mm, closed |
| Bare laptop slot | 26 mm; padding consumes this space |
| Reference rear gap | 40 mm from the wall |
| Fans | Two 120 × 120 × 25–27 mm fans |
| Fan direction | 45° upward and toward the wall |
| Wall fasteners | Four; 324 mm between bolt lines and 210 mm vertically |
| Service clearance | About 110 mm above the hinge |
| Sliding interfaces | 0.30 mm nominal clearance |
| Duct bends | Three R12 internal blends and two R14 external blends per duct |

The rounded duct transitions connect the fixed inlet and outlet shapes. The sampled bend wall is at least 1.60 mm thick. These are geometry checks; they do not establish a measured cooling improvement.

## Print and assemble

Print one of each numbered part, 01–14. Preserve the supplied orientations. The cradles lie on their outer sides; the ducts print inlet-down. The trays print grille-down, the rails lip-down, and the pins button-down.

The included legacy coupon checks the retained key and pin interfaces. It does not validate the revised full-length fan-tray fit. The new complete Bambu projects place all fourteen parts on nine named plates.

The Bambu Studio projects use 0.20 mm layers, four walls, six top and bottom layers, and 20% gyroid infill. PLA Basic, PETG HF, and ASA are separate material choices. Use the project for your printer and filament.

Tree supports apply only to the two duct parts. They address the slot-roof edges identified during Bambu toolpath review. Remove the supports before fitting the fan trays. Check the full sliding channels for remaining support material. The other parts have supports disabled.

Dry-fit the joints before assembly. The duct and outlet-rail joints use adhesive; the fan trays, caps, and pins remain removable. Fit compliant pads to the actual laptop without blocking vents or ports. Select wall anchors for the wall construction and applied load.

## Development status

The owner reports physically printing and trying ducted hardware with a laptop; the preserved arms are specifically documented as printed. These newly prepared material and printer profiles have only virtual review. That earlier physical use does not validate a complete Revision H assembly or the new profiles.

Load capacity, warm creep, anchor capacity, adhesive performance, and cooling results have no published rating. PLA needs particular care near heat and under sustained load. Additional shell layers do not remove those limits.

The gallery shows CAD renders. The laptop is a dimensional envelope; the download does not include a laptop or fans. Revisions and physical test results will be recorded here. Fit reports are welcome; include your laptop dimensions, material, printer, and the revision used.

## Optional removable retention

The separate H-R1 accessory uses two external bars and two keepers to retain the duct and upper rail. It fits Rev H, not the modular M1.1 mount. It uses the existing arm windows and replaces adhesive retention at those two joints.

Removing one bar releases both the duct and the rail on that side. Remove the laptop and support both modules before removing a bar. Retention force, keeper life, and warm creep remain untested. Print its right-arm fit coupon before the four accessory parts.
