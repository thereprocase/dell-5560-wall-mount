Two Precision 5560 laptops, one shared plenum

REV A · 15° STACK

A two-up variant of the Minimalist M1.1 mount: two tilted cradles stacked at a 260 mm pitch, fed from a single rear plenum, with flat cheek shrouds closing the sides. Every part is sized to print on a 256 mm bed. Geometry, mass and bed fit are measured from the FreeCAD model. Nothing here has been printed, sliced or airflow-tested.

05 / Open decisions

3
  1. Single-laptop use. If a cradle is often empty, a blanking panel is needed or the shared plenum dumps its pressure through the open bay.
  2. Wall substrate. Bolt spacing is 316.4 mm, which does not meet 406.4 mm studs; a plywood backer board is the default.
  3. Fan count. Two 120 mm fans at the bottom, with ladder slots left free on the upper arms for a second pair if tier 2 runs warm.

Full reasoning, failure modes and the 0/10/15/20° trade-off table are in the design memo shipped inside the download package.
