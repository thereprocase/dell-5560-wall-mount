Tight Wall-Mount Two-Banger: same-orientation stack, 55° fans

WALL MOUNT · 10° STACK

Tight-to-the-wall edition of the Two-Banger rack. Both Dell Precision 5560s sit in the same orientation—undersides facing a 35 mm wall plenum, leaning 10° forward, stacked at a 212 mm vertical pitch so Tier 2's base tucks 15 mm below Tier 1's hinge and forces Tier 1's hot exhaust outboard. Two 140 × 140 × 25 mm fans bolt onto a solid 12 mm 50° fan plate (printed in two halves, glued at X = 0) with M4 heat-set inserts in its ends taking M4 screws from the sides of the lower wall panels. Full-height 18 mm side cheeks and 26 mm gusseted inboard seats capture both laptops laterally; four flush 1 mm 2-color inlay panels with 1 × 1 mm V-grooves print flat on the bed with zero bridging.

05 / Open questions

DECISIONS
  • Wall substrate: Horizontal anchor spacing is 337.4 mm (misses 16-in / 406.4 mm studs)—mount a plywood backer board across studs or use rated hollow-wall toggle anchors.
  • Tier 1 exhaust vs. Tier 2 intake: Both laptops share one open wall plenum; if Tier 2 runs warm from Tier 1 hinge exhaust, add a simple sheet deflector at Z = 230 mm.
  • Overhead lift clearance: Allow ~45 mm above Tier 2's hinge (Z ≈ 520 mm total) for hand removal.
