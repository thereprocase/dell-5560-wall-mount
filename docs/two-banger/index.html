Two-Banger: two 5560s, two 140 mm fans, four printed parts

GREENFIELD · 14° A-FRAME

A desk-standing, back-to-back A-frame rack for two Dell Precision 5560s and two standard 140 × 140 × 25 mm fans. Both laptops lean inward at 14°, undersides facing a shared wedge plenum fed straight up by the two fans sitting flat at the bottom. Four support-free PETG parts bolt together with ten M4 screws and eight fan screws—no glue, no splice keys, no keeper pins.

05 / Open questions

DECISIONS
  • Rubber feet: Add 4× adhesive silicone bumpons under the end-frame feet if used on a slick desk.
  • Single-laptop mode: If one bay is frequently empty, add a flat PETG blanking plate or expect half the plenum pressure to vent through the open side.
  • Top tie bar: Not included so the rack stays at 4 parts and leaves the hinge exhaust unobstructed; verify frame rigidity once bolted together.
