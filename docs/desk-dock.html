The geometry comes directly from the D5 CAD assembly. Explode to see how the pieces fit together.

Exploded offsets illustrate assembly; they are not a fastening or assembly-order specification.

01 / GUIDANCE

Rest on the case.

A 2° lean, flared fixed cheeks and profiled hinge-end seats guide the laptop down, then 18 mm toward the far-side plug. Rubber feet have separate clearance envelopes.

02 / COOLING

Keep the intake open.

Bottom-pan grilles draw room air. The plenum collects hinge exhaust and routes it to two 120 × 15 mm fans discharging 15° above the desk. Added ribs leave 90.1% net free area over the intake region.

03 / ALIGNMENT

Tune it after printing.

Remove the cover to inspect separate height, lateral and depth adjustments. An independent chassis stop sets the final position; the USB-C plug is not the bearing stop.

CAD motion and clearance study. Physical fit, fastening, cooling and noise still need prototype validation. Download D5 review package ↓
